Abridged Life Table Constructor

Generate a Chiang-type period life table and survival curve from age-specific central mortality rates. Calculations stay in your browser; no account or server is required.

Mortality data

Age is the start of each interval, mx is the central death rate per person-year, and ax is mean years lived in that interval by those dying. Blank ax defaults to half the interval width. The final age interval is open-ended and requires mx > 0.

Method and assumptions. For closed intervals, qx = n·mx / [1 + (n − ax)·mx], capped at 1; Lx = n·lx+n + ax·dx. The final open interval uses qx = 1 and Lx = lx/mx. Reported values are period-life-table estimates, not individual survival predictions. Confidence intervals are not provided because mortality rates alone do not establish sampling uncertainty. Do not enter personally identifying information.
